# Product & Data Science Learning Track

A practical learning record for senior product analytics / product data science roles, grounded in e-commerce, retail SaaS, and fintech experience.

## Goal

Turn existing analytics leadership experience into interview-ready, portfolio-backed product decision-making capability: define products, specify metrics, diagnose funnels, design experiments, and communicate decisions.

## Learning sequence

| Phase | Focus | Evidence to add |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | AI & Data Product Management course | Course review and lesson notes |
| 1 | Product definition and metric trees | One metric tree per target domain |
| 2 | Funnels and diagnosis | Funnel analysis case study |
| 3 | Experimentation and causal inference | A/B test design and analysis case |
| 4 | Methodologies and execution | Product requirements, prioritisation, roadmap, operating cadence |
| 5 | Portfolio and interviews | Three polished, domain-specific case studies |

## Working rhythm

For each topic, capture:

1. **Concept** — concise definition and decision use.
2. **Framework** — reusable template or checklist.
3. **Applied case** — e-commerce, retail SaaS, or fintech scenario.
4. **Interview story** — how to explain the approach, trade-offs, and outcome.
5. **Open questions** — gaps to revisit.
